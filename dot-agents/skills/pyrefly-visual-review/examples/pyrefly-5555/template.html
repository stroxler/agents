ArgMap maps source arguments to matched parameter types and records missing required parameters. call_errors is mutated separately: it reports invalid calls and helps eliminate overload candidates. Returning an ArgMap does not mean the call is valid.

What this teaches about the diff
Two different facts were conflated.
matched = false means this splat has matched no positional parameter. It does not mean this splat must supply an argument.
Review focus · intentionally small
Keep the visual contract local.
  • Express the changed gate as one predicate; keep the minimum-length invariant in its comment.
  • Test fixed prefix and suffix guards, plus the overload return-type consequence.
  • The matched shortcut leaves a preexisting fixed-tail hole; don't mistake it for a new regression.
